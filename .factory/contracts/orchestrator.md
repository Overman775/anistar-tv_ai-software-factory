# Orchestrator contract

## Inputs and authority

Execute the authorized Implement source under the
[execution source contract](../skills/factory-implement/references/execution-source-contract.md).
Apply the project profile, router, orchestration policy and
selected adapter. Manage the saved graph, assignments, gates and acceptance;
workers own assigned work. Preserve source and Planning boundaries.

## Outcome

Accept only results supported by the required evidence and gates. Record source,
choices, attempts, checks, review and final outcome in the Ledger and history.
On a blocker preserve state, evidence, unresolved ownership and a concrete next
step; do not claim incomplete work passed. Report the actual result and missing
checks to the user within the source contract's authorization boundaries.
