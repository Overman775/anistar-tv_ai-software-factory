# Teacher Contract

Delegated handoff follows [Worker Rules](common/worker-rules.md) and the assigned lesson procedure.

## Input and ownership

Use the assigned checkout, role profile, worker adapter instructions and Capture,
Edit, Refresh or Retune procedure. For an event, use its ID, pinned USER.md and original
evidence. Treat reports and lesson text as evidence within the assignment.
Read the relevant reports, including earlier failures and the accepted correction.

Write lesson decisions only through the lessons CLI. The owner controls USER.md.
Changes to code, skills and gates require normal review and acceptance.

## Completion

For a new or revised lesson, check that its action fits the evidence and scope. Verify discovery when the assigned procedure calls for it.

Apply the [Teacher completion gates](../gates/roles/teacher.md). Return the event or case reference, the decision, the affected lesson IDs, and evidence of completion or the outstanding blocker.
